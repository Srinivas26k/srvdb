Utilities run grids, plants and assets with decades of documentation, often at remote sites.

## The challenge

- Procedures and asset records are scattered.
- Similar past outages hold the fastest fix.
- Field sites have limited connectivity.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Procedure and asset search** for control rooms and crews.
- **Similar-outage retrieval** from past event reports.
- **Offline on tablets and gateways**, crash-safe at remote sites.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Procedure Finder](/srvdb/industries/energy-utilities/procedure-finder/) | Operators find the right procedure from a description. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [Outage Analyzer](/srvdb/industries/energy-utilities/outage-analyzer/) | Finds similar past outages and their causes. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Inspection Image Matcher](/srvdb/industries/energy-utilities/inspection-image-matcher/) | Matches drone and field inspection images to known defects. |
| 4 · Expert | [Field Operations Agent](/srvdb/industries/energy-utilities/field-operations-agent/) | An offline agent for crews with procedures, asset history and notes. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Grid Analog Forecaster](/srvdb/industries/energy-utilities/grid-analog-forecaster/) | Finds historical analogues of current weather and load patterns. |
