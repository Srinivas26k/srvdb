Law firms and legal teams hold privileged documents that must never leave their control.

## The challenge

- Finding a clause like the one in front of you means reading hundreds of contracts.
- Relevant precedent is phrased differently from the question.
- Client confidentiality rules out many cloud tools.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Clause similarity:** embed every clause; find the closest wording across all past contracts.
- **Precedent retrieval:** search case summaries by meaning.
- **Privilege stays local:** the index is a folder on the firm's machines.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Clause Finder](/srvdb/industries/legal/clause-finder/) | Finds the closest clauses your firm has already used, across every contract. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [Contract Q&A](/srvdb/industries/legal/contract-qa/) | Ask questions about a contract set and get answers with clause citations. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Playbook Deviation Detector](/srvdb/industries/legal/playbook-deviation-detector/) | Flags clauses that drift from your negotiation playbook. |
| 4 · Expert | [E-discovery Assistant](/srvdb/industries/legal/ediscovery-assistant/) | Deduplicates, clusters and prioritises review sets by meaning. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Air-gapped Investigation Suite](/srvdb/industries/legal/air-gapped-investigation-suite/) | Offline analysis of highly sensitive investigation documents. |
