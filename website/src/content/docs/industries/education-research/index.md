Students and researchers need to search papers, notes and course material by meaning, often on their own laptops.

## The challenge

- Keyword search misses papers that use different terms.
- Course material is spread across files and years.
- Not every institution allows uploading material to cloud AI.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Paper search by meaning** over a personal or lab library.
- **Study assistant** that answers from course notes with citations.
- **Runs on a laptop**, offline, installed with pip.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Paper Search](/srvdb/industries/education-research/paper-search/) | Search a personal or lab library of papers by meaning. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [Study Assistant](/srvdb/industries/education-research/study-assistant/) | Answers questions only from the student's own course material. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Reviewer Matcher](/srvdb/industries/education-research/reviewer-matcher/) | Matches papers or proposals to suitable reviewers. |
| 4 · Expert | [Research Group Brain](/srvdb/industries/education-research/research-group-brain/) | A shared, searchable memory for a lab: notes, data descriptions, figures. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Scientific Similarity Lab](/srvdb/industries/education-research/scientific-similarity-lab/) | Similarity search over spectra, molecules and other scientific data. |
