Aerospace runs on manuals, maintenance records, safety reports and certification evidence, much of it export-controlled.

## The challenge

- Technicians search large manual sets under time pressure.
- Similar past findings across a fleet are hard to connect.
- Export-controlled data cannot go to cloud services.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Manual and bulletin search** with citations to the exact task.
- **Similar-finding retrieval** across maintenance records.
- **Offline and local:** suitable for controlled environments.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Maintenance Knowledge Assistant](/srvdb/industries/aerospace/maintenance-knowledge-assistant/) | Cited answers from manuals and service bulletins for technicians. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [Safety Report Analyzer](/srvdb/industries/aerospace/safety-report-analyzer/) | Clusters safety reports and links similar events. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Fleet Finding Matcher](/srvdb/industries/aerospace/fleet-finding-matcher/) | Matches new maintenance findings to similar findings across the fleet. |
| 4 · Expert | [Borescope Image Search](/srvdb/industries/aerospace/borescope-image-search/) | Find similar borescope images and the findings they led to. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Mission Knowledge System](/srvdb/industries/aerospace/mission-knowledge-system/) | Offline mission and ground-segment knowledge for space operations. |

:::caution
SrvDB is not certified software. Use it for decision support and knowledge retrieval, never in flight-critical or certified systems.
:::
