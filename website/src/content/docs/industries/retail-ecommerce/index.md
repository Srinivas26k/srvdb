Shoppers describe what they want in their own words; catalogues are organised by SKU.

## The challenge

- Keyword search fails on descriptive queries ("warm waterproof jacket for kids").
- "More like this" needs similarity, not categories.
- In-store kiosks and apps may run with poor connectivity.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Semantic product search** over product descriptions and images (CLIP-style embeddings).
- **Recommendations** with one `search` on an item's own vector.
- **Works offline** on kiosks and in mobile back-ends.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Semantic Product Search](/srvdb/industries/retail-ecommerce/semantic-product-search/) | Shoppers describe what they want and find it. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [Shopping Assistant](/srvdb/industries/retail-ecommerce/shopping-assistant/) | Answers product questions from your catalogue and reviews. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Listing Guardian](/srvdb/industries/retail-ecommerce/listing-guardian/) | Detects duplicate and counterfeit-looking listings. |
| 4 · Expert | [Multimodal Kiosk](/srvdb/industries/retail-ecommerce/multimodal-kiosk/) | In-store kiosk that searches by text or photo. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Edge Recommender](/srvdb/industries/retail-ecommerce/edge-recommender/) | "More like this" recommendations running on store or app hardware. |
