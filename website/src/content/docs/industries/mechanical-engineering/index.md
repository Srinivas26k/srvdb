Engineering knowledge lives in drawings, standards, test reports and design reviews collected over decades.

## The challenge

- Teams redesign parts that already exist under another number.
- Lessons from past failure analyses are forgotten.
- Standards and test reports are hard to search by meaning.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Part and design search** by functional description.
- **Failure-analysis retrieval:** find past investigations that match a new failure.
- **Runs on engineering workstations**, offline, with no server to manage.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Part Finder](/srvdb/industries/mechanical-engineering/part-finder/) | Find existing parts by describing what they do. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [FMEA Assistant](/srvdb/industries/mechanical-engineering/fmea-assistant/) | Drafts and reviews FMEAs grounded in past FMEAs and failures. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Vibration Signature Matcher](/srvdb/industries/mechanical-engineering/vibration-signature-matcher/) | Matches vibration signatures to known fault patterns. |
| 4 · Expert | [Geometric Design Search](/srvdb/industries/mechanical-engineering/geometric-design-search/) | Search CAD models by shape using 3D shape embeddings. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Simulation Lookup](/srvdb/industries/mechanical-engineering/simulation-lookup/) | Find the nearest previously simulated cases before running a new simulation. |
