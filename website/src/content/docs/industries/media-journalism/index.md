Newsrooms sit on decades of archives and sensitive source material.

## The challenge

- Finding earlier coverage of a story means guessing the right keywords.
- Leaked or source documents must not go to third-party services.
- Fact-checking needs the original passage, fast.

## Why SrvDB fits

- **Archive search by meaning** across years of articles.
- **Source protection:** sensitive document sets are indexed on an offline machine.
- **Passage-level results** with the article and date as metadata.

## Five projects, five levels

Each project has its own page: the problem, how SrvDB powers it, what changes, architecture, use cases, core code and limits.

| Level | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Beginner | [Archive Search](/srvdb/industries/media-journalism/archive-search/) | Search decades of articles by meaning. |
| 2 · Intermediate | [Fact-check Assistant](/srvdb/industries/media-journalism/fact-check-assistant/) | Matches claims against previously fact-checked claims and sources. |
| 3 · Advanced | [Narrative Tracker](/srvdb/industries/media-journalism/narrative-tracker/) | Clusters stories and posts into narratives. |
| 4 · Expert | [Investigation Workbench](/srvdb/industries/media-journalism/investigation-workbench/) | Isolated, multimodal workspace per investigation. |
| 5 · Frontier | [Secure Leak Analysis](/srvdb/industries/media-journalism/secure-leak-analysis/) | Air-gapped analysis of large leaked datasets. |
